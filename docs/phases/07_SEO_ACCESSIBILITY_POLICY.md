# Phase 7 — SEO, accessibility and truthful customer policy

**Status:** technical SEO built; customer policy remains an unapproved draft and is not published.

Local evidence (2026-10-06): canonical URLs on home, category and product pages; `noindex` on filtered variants, test fixtures, bag, checkout, order status, test wallet, error and admin pages; Product/Offer JSON-LD with BDT, `UsedCondition` and truthful `InStock`/`OutOfStock`/`SoldOut` (no ratings, GTINs or policy markup); `sitemap.xml` from published non-test pieces with `lastmod` from a trigger-maintained `products.updated_at`; `robots.txt` links the sitemap. Rich Results Test, Search Console and Lighthouse checks need a deployed site.

## Indexing and content contract

| Surface                                                                    | Required behavior                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Home/shop/real categories                                                  | SSR HTML, crawlable links, unique title/description, one clear heading and canonical URL. Do not create empty category SEO pages.                                                                    |
| Product                                                                    | Permanent slug (redirect on typo correction), actual description/brand/measurements/condition/photos and image alt text. Meaningful sold products return 200 with `SoldOut` and no purchase control. |
| Draft/unpublished/missing product                                          | True 404/410; a D1 read failure is an error/503, not an indexable empty shop.                                                                                                                        |
| Cart/checkout/payment return/order status/admin/search/filter combinations | Noindex and no customer data in metadata, JSON-LD, sitemap or public caches. Do not rely on `robots.txt` to protect private data.                                                                    |

Generate `sitemap.xml` from published indexable URLs with truthful `lastmod`, link it from `robots.txt`, and keep useful sold pages discoverable. The canonical storefront origin is `https://dathrift.shop`; redirect `www.dathrift.shop` after separately approved DNS/deployment work. Use real content for title/description/Open Graph/Twitter cards, canonical links and alt text. English is the launch language; add `bn-BD`/`hreflang` only after human-reviewed Bangla translations. Product/Offer JSON-LD uses BDT, actual brand when known, `UsedCondition`, and truthful `InStock`/`SoldOut`; no fabricated reviews, ratings, GTINs, discounts or policy data. Add organization/shipping/returns markup only after underlying policy approval. [Google merchant listing guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/merchant-listing) · [noindex guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/block-indexing)

Serve responsive, correctly sized photos with stable dimensions, descriptive filenames/alt text and lazy loading below the fold. Prioritize the first product image. Cache versioned assets but never checkout/payment responses; a cached stock label is not inventory enforcement.

## Policy draft — not approved for publication

> Every dathrift.shop piece is pre-loved and one of a kind. We photograph and describe its condition, including visible wear or flaws, and provide garment measurements so you can choose carefully. Each piece can be purchased only once. If an item becomes unavailable before your payment starts, we will ask you to review your cart rather than charge you for a substitute.
>
> Delivery costs **৳80 for eligible Dhaka city addresses** and **৳130 elsewhere in supported Bangladesh areas**, charged once per order. The eligible Dhaka-city areas will be listed before checkout. Your full delivery charge and order total will be shown before you pay. We will confirm dispatch and provide tracking/contact details when available. **Dispatch/delivery timeframes require courier approval.**
>
> If we send the wrong item, an item arrives damaged in transit, or its condition materially differs from what we disclosed, contact **[approved support channel]** with your order reference and photos. We will review the issue and arrange the appropriate remedy under our final policy and applicable law. **Report window, change-of-mind/fit terms, return shipping responsibility and refund timing remain to be approved.**

Steadfast is the intended courier. Its [coverage](https://www.steadfast.com.bd/coverage) is broad, but [off-grid terms](https://steadfast.com.bd/terms-and-condition) and [route/weight-based pricing](https://steadfast.com.bd/pricing) mean this plan must not promise every address at the same cost or timeframe yet. Owner/operations/legal must approve exact Dhaka-area list, merchant/pickup terms, off-grid exceptions, who absorbs any difference between customer fees and courier charges, delivery estimates, support contact, return window/evidence, fit/change-of-mind treatment, return shipping, refund method/timing under bKash terms and applicable Bangladesh law before public claims or structured data. This text is a proposal, not legal advice or final terms. [Bangladesh Digital Commerce Operation Guidelines](https://mincom.gov.bd/pages/static-pages/694032dc35ce18e1c0563903)

## Test-first slices and exit gate

Red-test rendered HTML metadata/canonical/JSON-LD/sitemap/robots/noindex, sold-versus-draft behavior and private-data absence. Inspect HTML with JavaScript disabled, validate structured data, check desktop/mobile visual and accessibility behavior including keyboard, screen reader and reduced motion. Before launch, run Google's Rich Results Test, submit sitemap and inspect sample URLs in Search Console, check mobile Lighthouse/Core Web Vitals and re-check after deployment. **Exit:** actual rendered pages and approved policy/content pass; no invented claims.
